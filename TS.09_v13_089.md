## 11 Bluetooth Interface Usage Test

This section is designed to test the effect of Bluetooth accessories on the standard operation of a terminal. Clearly the tests are only applicable to a terminal that supports Bluetooth and specifically supports the accessories indicated in the following subsections.

Record the Bluetooth standard version number used on the results sheet.

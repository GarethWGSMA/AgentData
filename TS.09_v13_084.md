## 10 Application software test

Suggested standard applications are as follows. These have been chosen as the applications most commonly provided on terminals at present. Clearly if a terminal does not support the application, the corresponding test is not required.

These are activities that do not require active data transfer channel. For these tests the terminal shall be in idle mode as far as air interface activities are concerned (either WCDMA or GSM/GPRS according to the type of terminal)

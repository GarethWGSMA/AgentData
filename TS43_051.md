## 3.3 Entitlement Modes of VoWiFi Client

To simplify the description of the client’s behaviour with respect to the VoWiFi entitlement configuration, a set of “VoWiFi entitlement modes” for the client is defined, each with specific expectations on the client side.

The relationship between the values of the VoWiFi entitlement parameters and the VoWiFi entitlement modes are shown in Table 20.

| VoWiFi Entitlement Parameters | VoWiFi Entitlement Parameters | VoWiFi Entitlement Parameters | VoWiFi Entitlement Parameters | VoWiFi Entitlement mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entitlement<br>Status | ProvStatus | TC_Status | AddrStatus | VoWiFi Entitlement mode |
| INCOMPATIBLE | Any | Any | Any | Cannot be offered |
| DISABLED | Any | At least one is NOT AVAILABLE | At least one is NOT AVAILABLE | Service Data Missing |
| DISABLED | Any | At least one is IN PROGRESS | At least one is IN PROGRESS | Service Data being Updated |
| DISABLED | NOT PROVISIONED, IN PROGRESS | AVAILABLE or NOT REQUIRED | AVAILABLE or NOT REQUIRED | Service being Provisioned |
| PROVISIONING | Any | Any | Any | Service being Provisioned |
| ENABLED | PROVISIONED or<br>NOT REQUIRED | AVAILABLE or NOT REQUIRED | AVAILABLE or NOT REQUIRED | Can be activated |

Table 20. VoWiFi Entitlement Modes

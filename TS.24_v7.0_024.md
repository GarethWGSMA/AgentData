## A.1.4 Further Analysis

TIS: -83.0

TRP & TIS Measurement Data for Mid/High Bands
: Received measurement data in dBm for smartwatches tested for the LTE bands B3, B7 and B1 with CTIA forearm phantom, defined for the frequency range 0.3 – 3 GHz

After applying the CDF function, the following values in dBm for the LTE bands B3, B7 and B1 have been aligned on:

LTE-B3	TRP: 11.5

TIS: -86.0

LTE-B7	TRP: 11.5

TIS: -86.0

LTE-B1	TRP: 11.5

TIS: -86.0

The values for the bands that were not measured are derived from the values of the measured bands (see Table 17 and Table 18 ).

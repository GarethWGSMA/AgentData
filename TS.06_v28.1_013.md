## 5.1.1 Type Allocation Code - 8 digits

The TAC identifies the type of the ME. It consists of two parts. The first part (NN) defines the RB allocating the TAC and the second part (XXXXXX) defines the ME Model.

The following allocation principles apply:

- Each ME Model SHALL have a unique TAC.
- More than one TAC MAY be allocated to an ME Model. This may be done for instance to permit the production of more than 1 million units.
- The TAC shall uniquely identify an ME Model.
- Where there is more than one Type Allocation Holder for an ME Model then the TAC shall be different.

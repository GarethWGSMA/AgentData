## 5.1.2 Reporting Body Identifier (NN) – 2 digits

The first two digits of the TAC are the RB Identifier. These digits indicate which RB issued the IMEI. See Annex A for the valid IMEI RB Identifiers.

The following RB Identifier allocation principles apply:

- The GSMA shall coordinate the allocation of the first 2 digits to RB Identifier.
- The RB Identifier shall uniquely identify the RB.

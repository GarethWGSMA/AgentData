## 7.2.2 Card Emulation mode requirements

All generic device requirements are applicable in addition to below specific requirements for Android.

| TS26_NFC_REQ_193 | This requirement is applicable from Android 10 onwards.<br><br>The device SHALL declare FEATURE_NFC_OFF_HOST_CARD_EMULATION_UICC |
| --- | --- |
| TS26_NFC_REQ_194 | This requirement is applicable from Android 10 onwards.<br><br>The device MAY declare FEATURE_NFC_OFF_HOST_CARD_EMULATION_ESE |

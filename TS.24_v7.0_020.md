## A.1.1 Test Methodology

Device selection was based on a random selection of commercially available devices at the time of measurement, typically these devices are from Tier 1 OEMs and are their Flagship or high-volume selling products.

The tests were carried out in an independent antenna lab, accredited by both CTIA (e.g., a CTIA Certification Authorized Test Lab) and GCF (Global Certification Forum).

The specific test methodologies used, followed the recommendations issued by CTIA.

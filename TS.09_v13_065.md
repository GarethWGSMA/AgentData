## 4.2 Battery Life Time

The recommended battery lifetime measure is the following:

Battery lifetime is quoted from current measurements in section 3 and battery capacity measurements in section 4

To obtain a battery lifetime in terms of hours:

- Divide the battery capacity by the average current consumption

To obtain a battery lifetime in terms of data transfer:

- Divide the battery capacity by the average current consumption
- Divide the number of hours by 0.1666 (=10 minutes) and multiply by the data transferred in 10 minutes

To obtain the current consumption per MB for USB data modem (in mAh per MB):

- Multiply the average current consumption by 0.1666 (=10 minutes) and divide by the data transferred in 10 minutes

## 10.3.1 Online Verification

The received documents will be checked against various government owned websites and online resources to ensure the authenticity of the TAC applicant company and the verification of the company principals.

Stage 2 - Taxation Requirements

The GSMA levies an administration charge for the allocation of TACs, as detailed in the Terms & Conditions, for more details please refer to section 11.3 of GSMA PRD TS.16. The Indian authorities require companies registered in India to pay tax on the charges collected, this tax must be added to the charges detailed in the Terms & Conditions.  Tax required shall be paid to the Indian Government and not provided to the GSMA.

## 11.4 DUT in BT discovery mode – Standby Time

- This scenario shall be run on top of a Standby Time scenario (refer section 5).

Bluetooth is enabled on the DUT side but remains unconnected with other devices throughout the test cycle. There shall be no other Bluetooth device in range. The DUT shall be kept in invisible mode while the test is executed.

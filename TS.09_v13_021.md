## 2.5.3 WCDMA (GSM/GPRS) Dual Mode Standby Parameter

In this scenario the DUT is camped on the WCDMA according to section 2.4.1 with the addition of 16 GSM/GPRS neighbour cells in the BA_LIST according to section 2.3.2, with frequencies as defined in Annex A.21.2.

NOTE:	If the test equipment does not support 16+16 neighbour cell configuration, then choose the best fit possible and note in Annex B “Pro-forma tables”.

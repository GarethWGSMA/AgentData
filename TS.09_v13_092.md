## 11.3 Headset – Music Player

- This scenario shall be run on top of the Music Playback scenario (refer section 10.1).
- The test shall be run with a commercially available Bluetooth certified headset. The test report should specify if the connection between Phone and Headset is an EDR level or non-EDR level connection. Using a BT A2DP headset with optimum bit rate can lower the power consumed.

## 11.1.1 Documents Required for Registration

| Pvt. Ltd / Ltd Company / Partnerships |
| --- |
| Enterprise business license (GD)<br>Company Registration Number (GD)<br>Registered capital (GD)<br>Scope of business (GD)<br>Results of the annual check-ups (GD)<br>ISO9001:2000 quality system Certification (GD)<br>ISO9001:2000 quality system Certification number (GD)<br>Brand Registration Certificate (GD)<br>Brand Registration Certificate Number (GD)<br>GSMA Manufacturers Terms and Conditions (signed and dated) |

The brand owner will also be required to send photocopies of the Government issued documents (marked as GD) listed above to the Reporting Body.
